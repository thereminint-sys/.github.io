The boy who lost his father has returned as a hunter—to drive an iron stake through the hearts of the wolves.

A lawless world ruled by the Werewolf Syndicate. Human dignity has been trampled beneath the feet of beasts, and ‘The Legion,’ the world's last hope for salvation, faded into history 20 years ago following the gruesome death of its leader.

An era of flawless control and absolute despair. But as the lords of the night revel in their dark triumph, an unknown executioner emerges from the shadows, brutally hunting down the werewolves.

He is Brandon, the son of the fallen resistance leader. Having endured two decades of hell to forge himself into a lethal wolf tracker, he returns to unleash a blood-soaked retribution upon the realm of beasts.

The night the roles of predator and prey are reversed—who will survive?!
